# Week 4: Prompt Engineering

## Employee Attendance System

This document includes three prompts that can be used in the Employee Attendance System. Each prompt has a specific purpose, such as summarizing daily attendance, explaining late arrivals, and preparing monthly attendance reports.

The prompts are written using four main parts: Role, Context, Task, and Output Format.

---

## 1. Daily Attendance Summary

**Role:**

You are an assistant that helps employees understand their daily attendance.

**Context:**

The official working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The allowed late arrival period is 15 minutes.

An employee has the following attendance records:

- Check-in: 9:10 AM
- Check-out: 5:00 PM

**Task:**

Calculate the employee's working hours and check whether the employee arrived on time or within the allowed late period.

**Output Format:**

Write a short and clear summary using bullet points.

---

## 2. Late Arrival Explanation

**Role:**

You are an attendance assistant that explains late arrivals to employees.

**Context:**

- Official start time: 9:00 AM
- Allowed late period: 15 minutes
- Employee check-in time: 9:25 AM

**Task:**

Calculate how late the employee arrived and explain whether the allowed late period was exceeded.

**Output Format:**

Give a simple explanation in two or three sentences.

---

## 3. Monthly Attendance Report

**Role:**

You are an assistant that prepares employee attendance reports.

**Context:**

The employee has the following attendance records for one month:

- Working days: 22
- Attendance days: 20
- Late arrivals: 3
- Absences: 2
- Official working hours: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

**Task:**

Review the attendance information and prepare a short monthly report.

**Output Format:**

Present the attendance statistics as bullet points, followed by a short conclusion.

---

## Conclusion

These prompts will help the attendance system provide clear daily summaries, explain late arrivals, and generate monthly attendance reports.
