# Employee Attendance Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the proposed attendance rules for the Employee Attendance System.

These rules help organize employee attendance, working hours, late arrivals, and temporary leaves.

**Note:** This document contains proposed sample rules. The final policy must be reviewed and approved by the supervisor or company management.

## 2. Working Hours Policy

The proposed working hours are:

- Start Time: 9:00 AM
- End Time: 5:00 PM
- Total Working Hours: 8 hours per day

The official working days and hours must be confirmed by the company.

Employees are expected to check in at the beginning of their working day and check out at the end of their working day.

## 3. Late Arrival Policy

An employee is considered late when their check-in time is later than the official working start time.

The system may use a proposed 10-minute grace period.

For example:

- Official Start Time: 9:00 AM
- Grace Period: 15 minutes
- Check-in at 9:10 AM: Within the grace period
- Check-in at 9:20 AM: Late arrival

The system should record the actual check-in time and calculate the late duration.

Any disciplinary action must be reviewed by the company management and must not be decided automatically by the system.

## 4. Break and Temporary Leave Policy

Employees may use the Temporary Leave feature to record a short period away from work.

When requesting a temporary leave, the employee should:

- Record the leave time.
- Enter the expected return time.
- Provide a reason if required by the company.
- Record the actual return-to-work time.

The system should calculate the temporary leave duration using the recorded timestamps.

Temporary leave should be recorded separately from a full-day absence.

Approval requirements and allowed leave durations must be confirmed by the company.

## 5. Attendance Records and Privacy

The system should:

- Store accurate check-in and check-out timestamps.
- Keep attendance records organized by employee and date.
- Allow employees to view their own attendance records.
- Restrict access to company-wide reports to authorized administrators.
- Protect employee information from unauthorized access.

## 6. Policy Review

This policy is a proposed sample for Project 2.

All working hours, grace periods, leave rules, and approval requirements must be reviewed and approved before being used as official company policy.
